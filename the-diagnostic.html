The Business Diagnostic & Roadmap Session

An X-ray for your business, not another round of guesswork

Not a motivational talk. Not advice to run ads when your margins are already leaking. An outside, objective pair of eyes that sits with your numbers and tells you the blunt truth.

What the session covers

This is a private, intensive, one-on-one consultation, either face to face or on a closed video call. We do three specific things.

01

The diagnosis

We strip down your books, your inventory flow, your pricing, and your daily operations. You'll get a direct answer on where your cash is disappearing and which customers or products are quietly robbing you.

02

The prescription

The exact structural, pricing, and operational adjustments you need to make to stop the bleeding, protect your cash, and recover trapped working capital.

03

The execution path

A crystal-clear, sequential roadmap in plain English: do this in week one, fix this in week two, renegotiate this supplier term immediately.

Who this is for

  • You run a business that looks successful from the outside, but you can't explain where the money actually goes.

  • You're carrying the accounting, the marketing, and the debt collection yourself, and the business stalls the moment you step away.

  • You're ready for a clinical, no-jargon action plan rather than another motivational conversation.

My role in this session is that of a specialist. I diagnose the disease, prescribe the exact cure, and hand you the treatment protocol. The execution is yours, though we can discuss implementation support afterward if it makes sense.
