Because you know the truth that nobody else sees

To your church, your friends, and your family, you have made it. You run a company, you employ staff, you have an office, a warehouse, or a store where goods move every day. Then you close your office door, sit behind your desk, and your chest begins to tighten.

  • It is the 23rd of the month, and you still don't know where next week's payroll is coming from.

  • Customers bought goods on credit three months ago and won't pay up, but your suppliers are blowing up your WhatsApp line demanding cash upfront.

  • The cost of diesel, clearing goods, logistics, and rent is climbing every single week. You are selling in millions of Naira, but when you check your corporate account, the balance is mocking you.

  • The staff you pay to help you act like spectators. If you don't show up in person to shout, monitor, or push, nothing moves. The business cannot run without your physical presence for three straight days.

You sit there thinking: “I am making sales. Money is entering this account. Why am I struggling like this? Where did all the money go?”

You are pouring water into a basket

Your business is not suffering because the economy is tough. Yes, inflation and exchange rates are rough, but other businesses in your exact same space are making clean profit and expanding with sanity. Your business is suffering because it has internal leaks. It rarely collapses overnight, it bleeds out slowly.

Your margins are fake

You think you made a 20% margin, but by the time you factor hidden logistics, bank charges, demurrage, damaged goods, and utility costs, you actually sold at a loss.

Your money is trapped in dead places

It's sitting in uncollected debts from friends and old customers, or parked in slow-moving stock that has been gathering dust on shelves for eight months.

The founder bottleneck

You are doing the job of the accountant, the marketing lead, the debt collector, and the manager. You are not running a company, you are running a high-risk daily struggle that will collapse the day you fall sick.

Praying about it won't fix bad numbers. Pushing harder and sleeping only 3 hours a night won't fix it either. When an engine is broken, stepping harder on the accelerator only blows the engine.
